### The Driver v1.10.0 - Reliability & Safety Release

- **The floating HUD now uses your real settings.** Fuel price, consumption, commission, zones and badge style set in the app now reach the HUD. Zones you tag from the HUD also appear in the app.
- **The badge shows net profit.** Green means profitable, yellow means a thin margin, red means a loss, and grey "؟" means the distance couldn't be read. A ★ marks favourite zones and ⛔ marks blocked zones. A favourite zone can no longer hide a losing ride.
- **Safer auto-accept.** It re-checks the request right before acting and taps the card's own Accept button. It never accepts when the distance wasn't read correctly, and it rejects blocked zones by default. A warning appears before you enable it.
- **The expanded card no longer blocks inDrive.** Tap outside it to close it. Moved badges stay where you put them.
- **A "Stop" button in the notification** turns the assistant off quickly.
- **Arabic digits (٠-٩) accepted in every field**, with clear validation messages and no pre-filled fake values.
- **Each vehicle keeps its own costs.** Fuel consumption is the average of your last refills. Spare parts change the wear rate only if you choose, and deleting a part undoes it.
- **Vehicles can be edited and deleted.** Trips, expenses and zones ask for confirmation before deletion.
- **The shift stays active** after the app is closed, with its duration shown.
- **Safe storage:** a corrupted record no longer wipes your history.
- **Faster, lighter release build** with a verified (SHA-256) in-app update.
