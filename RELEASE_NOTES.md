### The Driver v1.15.0 - Fair pickup pricing, passenger lists

- **Pickup kilometres are priced separately (on by default).** The km to the passenger now cost less than trip km in the fair price and the suggested bids:
  - pickup km = fuel + wear and maintenance + a share of inDrive's commission (50% of it by default) + its own profit per km (3 ج.م by default);
  - trip km stay as before (full commission + your profit target per km).
  - Settings → التسعير والتكاليف → "تسعير مسافة الوصول", with a worked example. Turn it off to go back to the old formula.
  - Badge colours follow the same fair price, so an offer at the fair price is no longer shown as a thin margin.
- **Favourite and blocked passengers.** Mark a passenger from the floating card over inDrive ("+ عميل مميز" / "+ عميل محظور") or in Settings → العملاء.
  - Blocked passengers' requests always show red, and auto-accept never accepts them.
  - Favourite passengers get a ★ on the badge.
  - Matching uses the name shown on the card; inDrive shows first names only, so blocking a common name blocks everyone with that name.
