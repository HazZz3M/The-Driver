### The Driver v1.13.0 - Clearer badge, fewer surprises

- **The floating badge is easier to read at a glance.**
  - "Excellent" rides now have a solid green badge, so they stand out more than ordinary profitable rides (before, "excellent" was the dimmer green).
  - Every badge has a small symbol in its corner: ✓ profitable, ! thin margin, ✕ loss. You don't have to rely on the colour alone.
  - Favourite zones show a blue ★ and no longer pulse in gold, which looked like the yellow "thin margin" colour.
  - Badge text is larger (never below 11sp).
- **No preset zones.** New installs start with empty favourite and blacklisted zones. Common areas are offered as one-tap suggestions on the Zones screen. Zones you already saved are kept.
- **Currency is written "ج.م" everywhere** (it was "EGP" in some places and "ج" in others).
- **Calculator:** picking a suggested bid no longer replaces the passenger's offer. A line under the bids compares the two. "Reset" can be undone.
- **Shift:** ending a shift can be undone from the message that appears.
- Small text in the app is now at least 12sp, and grey hint text has higher contrast.
