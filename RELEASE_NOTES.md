### The Driver v1.16.0 - Smart bids, pickup rules, tutorial

- **Smart bid circles.** On a request screen, a circle above each of inDrive's bid buttons shows your net profit for that price. The best one is filled and starred: the cheapest price that meets your target (not necessarily the highest). Settings → العلامة العائمة → المزايدة:
  - off / show circles (default) / show and tap the best one automatically (asks before turning on).
- **Pickup pricing rules.** Pickup pricing can now apply only when the pickup is long:
  - when the pickup is longer than the trip, and/or longer than a fixed number of km (any or both);
  - otherwise a short pickup is counted as cost only (default), free, or like trip km.
  - Settings → التسعير والتكاليف, with two worked examples.
- **Auto-accept: best request first (optional).** When several requests qualify at once, accept the best one, by profit per km, net profit, or margin %.
- **Tutorial.** A clear walkthrough on first launch (skippable), and any time from Settings → شرح التطبيق.
